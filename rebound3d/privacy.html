Rebound 3D - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026. Developer: TerraVectors. Contact: terravectors@gmail.com

What this version collects

Rebound 3D (package com.terravectors.rebound3d) is in testing with invited testers. From version 0.5.0 (5) it can show rewarded ads and connect to our game server for online play. From version 0.5.14 (14) it also connects to our server by itself while it is open, as described under "Game statistics". It has no Google Analytics and no accounts tied to your name. Your game progress stays on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, the ad is served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and ad-interaction events, under Google's own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Online play

When you play online, the game connects to our game server (a "relay"), which passes the moves between the players. The game sends the server a random number made on your phone (not your name, account or advertising ID; a new one is made if you reinstall) and the player name shown on the court. The server sees your IP address while you are connected, to deliver the game's messages.

Checking that the game is genuine

When a version of the game includes this check, the game asks Google Play (the Play Integrity service) to confirm that this is a genuine copy of the game on a genuine device. This happens when you open online play, and never for offline play. Google Play processes the game's package name, version and signing certificate, your Google Play license status for the game, and information that attests to your device. The result reaches us through our game server: whether Google Play recognizes the app, its license status and a device-integrity label. We use it only for security and to prevent cheating, not for ads and not to identify or follow you or your device. We do not store the token or Google's full answer. Our logs keep only the game's version and the result, without your random number, for up to 90 days. A signed pass tied to your random number and the game's version lets you play online for up to one hour without a new check. Depending on the version, a modified copy, or a device that does not pass the check (for example a rooted phone, a custom ROM, an emulator or an uncertified device), or a device where the check cannot be completed (for example without Google Play services), may be refused online play, and Google Play may not offer the game for installation on a device that does not pass. An installed game still opens and plays offline, and your progress stays on your device. We do not keep a list of refused players or devices.

Game statistics

From version 0.5.14 (14), while the game is open it tells our server that it is here (a random number made on your phone, the game's version and the open screen), receives the game's settings, and sends short game events: a screen or feature was opened, a match started or ended, credits, rewarded ads, a lost connection, a forced update, your age-mode answer and country, and that you shared the game and which way. They never include your name. The online court may run on a server in another country. Our server logs are kept for up to 14 days. Our statistics, with the random number, are kept for up to 90 days; any IP address or account identifier in them is removed after 14 days.

Sharing the game

If you are an adult player and use SHARE THE GAME, the store link carries a random code made on your phone, so that an install from a share can be told apart. In protected mode, or while your age is not known, the link has no code. The code is kept on your phone; our server receives only that you shared and which way (WhatsApp, copy or the phone's share sheet).

Google Play

Google Play may collect installation and crash information under Google's own privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

When a later version adds a new kind of data, accounts, purchases or game recordings, this page will be updated before that version is released, and it will list exactly what is collected and why.

Deleting your data

Your game progress is stored only on your device, and uninstalling the game removes it. To delete what our server holds about you, use "My data" in Settings > Privacy, or write to the contact address with the number shown there; we answer within 30 days. Server logs clear by themselves within 14 days. Data processed by Google AdMob is handled under Google's policies; write to the contact address above for any question. See also delete-data.html.
