Rebound 3D - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026. Developer: TerraVectors. Contact: terravectors@gmail.com

What this version collects

From version 0.5.0 (5), a test version for invited testers, Rebound 3D (package com.terravectors.rebound3d) can show rewarded ads and can connect to our game server for online play. It has no analytics and no accounts tied to your name. Your game progress stays on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, the ad is served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and ad-interaction events, under Google's own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Online play

When you play online, the game connects to our game server (a "relay"), which passes the moves between the players. The game sends the server a random number made on your phone (not your name, account or advertising ID; a new one is made if you reinstall) and the player name shown on the court. The server sees your IP address while you are connected, to deliver the game's messages; it does not store it.

Google Play

Google Play may collect installation and crash information under Google's own privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

When a later version adds online play, advertising or accounts, this page will be updated before that version is released, and it will list exactly what is collected and why.

Deleting your data

Your game progress is stored only on your device, and uninstalling the game removes it. Our server keeps nothing about you after you leave a game. Data processed by Google AdMob is handled under Google's policies; write to the contact address above for any question. See also delete-data.html.
