Rebound 3D - Delete your data

Rebound 3D (TerraVectors) has no accounts. Your game progress is on your device: to delete it, uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings. From version 0.5.14 (14) our server also holds statistics tied to a random number made on your phone. To delete them, use "My data" in Settings > Privacy, or write to terravectors@gmail.com with that number. We delete your statistics and answer within 30 days; server logs clear by themselves within 14 days.

Questions: terravectors@gmail.com
